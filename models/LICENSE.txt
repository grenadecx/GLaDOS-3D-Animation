GLaDOS.glb
==========

A glTF (.glb) conversion of "GladOS from Portal 2 Rigged Textured Updated"
by BlenderCranium, obtained from BlendSwap:

    https://blendswap.com/blend/4565

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC-BY-SA).
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/

Changes made to the original:
  - Exported from .blend to glTF binary (.glb).
  - Material colours are re-authored at runtime by src/model.ts; the blend's
    procedural materials did not survive the glTF export.
  - Unused material-preview objects are pruned at load time, and loose cable
    meshes are re-parented to the nearest bone. Both at runtime only — the
    .glb itself is unmodified geometry.
